# Problem brief

**Monday: Find it.** In 150 to 300 words, explain one real problem: who it hits and where, what your product will help them do, and what data is missing today. Write it for someone who has never been there, then paste it into the Build54 task.

## Copy this outline

**Title:** [the problem, the people and the place, in one line]

**The problem.** [Two or three sentences: what happens, how often, and what it costs people. Add a number if you have one, with its source.]

**Who it hits, and where.** [The people and the place. Be specific: a town, a market, a school, a route.]

**What our product will do.** With [product name], [who] can [do what], so that [what changes for them].

**What the data misses today.** [What isn't counted, which places, groups or months are missing, and why that matters to the people above.]

**The data we start with.** [Dataset name and link, from the starter list or your own.]

## A short example

Mbari is a made-up town, used here only to show the shape.

> **Title:** Mothers in Mbari walk to health posts that turn out to be closed.
>
> **The problem.** When a health post closes early or runs out of a vaccine, mothers only find out when they arrive, after up to two hours on foot. Many give up and miss the appointment.
>
> **Who it hits, and where.** Mothers with babies under one in the villages east of Mbari, where the nearest posts are furthest apart.
>
> **What our product will do.** With PostCheck, a mother can see on her phone which posts are open today and which vaccines they have, so she only makes the trip when it will work.
>
> **What the data misses today.** The public list of health posts shows where they are, but not opening hours, stock or staff. None of the posts east of the river has hours recorded.
>
> **The data we start with.** The healthsites.io list for our country, from the starter datasets.

## Check before you send it

- It is 150 to 300 words.
- Someone who has never been there would understand it.
- Every number has a source.
- It names one problem, not three.
- It has no names, phone numbers or exact addresses of private people.
